Our Story

Nestled in the heart of the countryside, Green Valley Farm has been a family-run agricultural paradise for over three generations. We're passionate about sharing the beauty of farm life and sustainable agriculture with visitors from around the world.

Our 100-acre organic farm offers a perfect blend of traditional farming practices and modern eco-tourism. Whether you're looking for a peaceful retreat, educational experience, or adventure-filled vacation, we have something special for everyone.

Organic Farming

  • Vegetable harvesting
  • Composting workshops
  • Seed planting sessions

Animal Care

Get up close with our friendly farm animals and learn about their care and importance in sustainable farming.

  • Cow milking experience
  • Feeding chickens & goats
  • Horse riding lessons

Farm-to-Table Dining

Enjoy delicious meals prepared with fresh ingredients straight from our farm to your table.

  • Cooking classes
